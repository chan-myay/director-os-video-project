## Replacement visual family

Use these seven kinds of coverage across the former dance slots:

1. **Winter outside the bar** — frozen lake, birch/spruce, snow, warm window.
2. **Mature emotional portrait** — wife in the same bar, mouth naturally
   closed, gaze and breath carrying the lyric.
3. **Adult band insert** — restrained professional musicians playing the song
   atmosphere; never a nightclub act.
4. **Small mature audience** — a few adults over 30 in shadow, listening
   quietly; never cheering, dancing or blocking the performer.
5. **Burmese architectural/material detail** — carved teak arch, lacquer,
   acheik textile, restrained gold geometry integrated naturally into the same
   Finnish bar.
6. **Clean nature memory** — snowfall, frozen water, winter branches and blue
   twilight used as emotional breath.
7. **Symbolic object action** — glass, rose, chair, clock, door bolt or curtain.

## Band rules

- Three or four adult musicians only.
- Piano or restrained keyboard, upright bass, soft brushed drums and optional
  Burmese harp/saung-inspired visual accent only when culturally credible.
- The band is background atmosphere, not a competing performance.
- No young idol band, nightclub stage, rock-show lighting or exaggerated
  playing.
- No generated singing by background people.

## Audience rules

- Sparse, mature adults only; no children.
- Seated or standing quietly at tables in soft shadow.
- Emotionally attentive, never clapping, dancing, filming on phones or staring
  theatrically.
- Keep faces secondary and non-identifiable.

## Burmese architecture rules

- Preserve the Finnish bar geography.
- Add restrained carved-teak arch geometry, lacquer panels, acheik textile,
  warm gold linework and handcrafted joinery.
- Do not replace the bar with a temple, pagoda, palace or generic pan-Asian
  fantasy set.
- No Buddha image, sacred manuscript text, fake Burmese writing or religious
  spectacle.
