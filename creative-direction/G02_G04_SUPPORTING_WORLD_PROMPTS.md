## G02 — restrained adult band in the same bar

`production/raw/batch-g/G02_restrained-adult-band_same-bar_take01.mp4`

```text
PHOTOREALISTIC LIVE-ACTION CINEMATIC ADULT BAND INSERT IN THE EXACT APPROVED
DARK TEAK FINNISH BAR.

Preserve the supplied bar architecture, crimson curtains, carved teak, liquor
shelves, winter window, amber practicals, polished floor and camera geography.
Do not redesign or replace the venue.

Three mature adult musicians over 35 perform with quiet professional restraint
in the background area of the same bar: soft brushed drums, upright bass and
restrained piano/keyboard. They play a slow, emotionally intense 75 BPM
heartbreak arrangement. No singer is visible. No one mouths lyrics. Movement is
small, musically believable and synchronized with a slow pulse, never
theatrical.

Camera: one stable 50mm medium-wide composition with an extremely slow lateral
drift. Warm amber/crimson light, cold winter-blue window separation, rich teak,
subtle haze and realistic skin. Mature cinematic drama, not a concert.

AVOID: young idol band, rock concert, nightclub, dancing, cheering, exaggerated
drumming, guitar solo, singer, visible lyric mouthing, karaoke, neon,
strobe lights, changing bar architecture, extra limbs, warped instruments,
text, captions and logos.
```
