## Camera and body rules for every performer shot

- Eye level or slightly above; never low-angle body emphasis.
- Portrait, medium-close or three-quarter framing.
- No rear view unless the face remains visible in a dignified three-quarter turn.
- Never frame butt, hips or chest as the subject.
- Natural over-40 face, pores, lines and adult proportions.
- Stable 50mm or 85mm portrait language; 35mm only for environmental medium.
- Slow push, slow lateral drift or locked frame. No orbit, whip or dance camera.
- Small authentic singing gestures. No choreography.

## Credit strategy

Do not generate all nine potential replacements immediately. First review the
existing Batch C clips in the new context. Generate only the first proven gap,
inspect it at normal speed, then continue one at a time.
