# Product Vision

Director OS is a world-class AI director platform for controlled music-video
and cinematic sequence preparation.

It is Burmese-first and global-ready. The first project proves the core
standard: a Burmese commercial music video with a real adult performer,
director-approved identity references, cultural art direction, renderer-neutral
shot packs, approval gates, and QC before any generation spend.

Director OS is not a toy generator. It is not prompt-only. It is not a random
clip machine.

## Product Thesis

Good AI video needs a director layer before a renderer layer.

Director OS organizes:

- Director bible
- Asset registry
- Identity, body, wardrobe, location, and culture locks
- Shot packs
- Reference library
- Motion reference library
- Renderer adapters
- Approval gates
- Spend gates
- QC scorecards
- Audit logs

## Target User

- Music video directors
- Independent filmmakers
- AI video producers
- Artists working in under-served languages and cultures
- Teams that need repeatable control instead of random prompt retries

## Burmese-First Positioning

The platform treats Burmese lyrics, pronunciation, cultural image references,
captioning, and music-video language as first-class product objects. Burmese is
not a translation afterthought.

## Product Promise

Turn messy creative evidence into controlled generation packages that a human
director can approve before spending money, uploading private media, or touching
a renderer.
