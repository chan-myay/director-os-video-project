# World-Class Bar

Director OS should hold every project to a world-class bar before generation.

## Cinematic Quality

- Clear shot purpose.
- Strong composition.
- Controlled lighting.
- Camera movement that supports the song.
- No random visual drift.

## Motion Realism

- Natural body mechanics.
- Clean hands, feet, legs, fabric, and contact points.
- Movement vocabulary fits the song.
- Normal-speed review required.

## Cultural Authenticity

- Burmese visual/cultural detail is deliberate.
- Avoid generic Asian substitution.
- Avoid K-pop, Chinese-lounge, karaoke, and stock nightclub drift unless a
  project explicitly asks for those.

## Director Control

- Every shot has intent, references, motion, camera, identity, body, location,
  negatives, and QC.
- Renderer prompts are outputs of the system, not the system itself.

## Repeatable Workflow

- Same evidence can compile to multiple renderer packages.
- Failures become rules.
- Approved references are tracked.
- Rejected references stay blocked.

## Hard Rejects

- Fake body.
- Fake lip-sync.
- Karaoke drift.
- Microphone-centered performance when not approved.
- Unapproved identity references.
- Child-containing sources.
- Random credit spending without approval.

## Real QC Gates

- Identity gate.
- Body/wardrobe gate.
- Location/culture gate.
- Motion gate.
- Face/lip exposure gate.
- Rights/privacy gate.
- Spend gate.
