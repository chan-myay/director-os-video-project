## Working Principle That Succeeded

Separate the work into stages:

1. Identity references.
2. Wardrobe/body references.
3. Stage/location references.
4. Motion/background plates.
5. Face correction.
6. Edit against the real song.
7. Lip-sync only after footage and face consistency are stable.
