# v2C QC Checklist

Use this only after v2C is explicitly rendered by the user or by an approved
future instruction.

## World / Direction

- [ ] The video still reads as the dark bar/winter world.
- [ ] The mood is rhythmic dark bar-stage performance, not lounge/karaoke.
- [ ] The red dress remains visually consistent enough for this diagnostic.
- [ ] The microphone appears only as an ending symbol, not performance center.

## Movement

- [ ] Less repeated front-facing dance than v2B.
- [ ] Entrance feels stronger because of surrounding cuts.
- [ ] Verse 1 has more body rhythm and less static face exposure.
- [ ] Chorus 1 feels stronger from D06/D04/D03 arrangement.
- [ ] Later verse does not collapse into static/object-only pacing.
- [ ] Final chorus has enough body movement to support the song energy.
- [ ] D05 feet/dress appears no more than two short times.

## Face / Lip Risk

- [ ] Batch B is never used as lead performer identity.
- [ ] Batch B faces are small, hidden, cut around, or not emphasized.
- [ ] Batch C is not overexposed.
- [ ] C05 is not used.
- [ ] Close/medium Batch C placeholders are understood as not final approval.
- [ ] Object cutaways hide weak face or lip moments.
- [ ] No visible generated singing is treated as final lip-sync.

## Edit / Story

- [ ] Batch A objects cover face-risk points without feeling random.
- [ ] Object story remains clear: glasses, chair, rose, clock, lock, snow, mic.
- [ ] Cut rhythm follows high-energy sections better than v2B.
- [ ] Reused D03/D04/D06 shots are separated enough to feel intentional.

## Decision After Review

- [ ] Keep EDL structure and proceed to render notes.
- [ ] Revise EDL before face correction.
- [ ] Mark specific shots for HiFiFace/UniFace later.
- [ ] Mark specific gaps that cannot be solved without future controlled
      generation.
