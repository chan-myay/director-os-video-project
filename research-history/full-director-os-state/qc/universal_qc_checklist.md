# Universal QC Checklist

Use after a future generation or render is explicitly approved and completed.

## Identity

- [ ] Performer resembles approved lead performer references.
- [ ] Face is small/shadowed/profile if not face-corrected.
- [ ] No rejected identity references used.
- [ ] No child-containing sources used.

## Body / Wardrobe

- [ ] Natural adult body proportions.
- [ ] No enlarged hips, butt, or model-body reshaping.
- [ ] Red dress remains consistent.
- [ ] Hands, legs, feet, and dress physics are clean.

## World

- [ ] Dark teak bar world is present.
- [ ] Winter window / amber-crimson lighting continuity holds.
- [ ] Burmese art/craft appears as integrated material detail.
- [ ] No wrong venue, piano, drums, band, crowd, or karaoke setup.

## Motion

- [ ] Movement is mature, elegant, and cinematic.
- [ ] No kung fu, martial arts, K-pop, TikTok, or vulgar nightclub movement.
- [ ] Body/stage/movement works without relying on face correction.

## Face / Lip

- [ ] No fake singing or fake lip-sync.
- [ ] No close clear face approved without correction review.
- [ ] Microphone is not performance center.

## Decision

- [ ] Select
- [ ] Reject
- [ ] Unknown / requires normal-speed review
